# 3D Printing Innovations: From Concrete to Dentures

The world of 3D printing is constantly evolving, and today's news headlines showcase some exciting developments and applications across various industries. Here are some of the most positive and uplifting stories from the world of 3D printing:

## UNIST Develops Ultrasound Technology for 3D Printed Concrete

[UNIST Uses Ultrasound to Tell When 3D Printed Concrete Can Take the Next Layer](https://3dprinting.com/)

Researchers at UNIST have developed an innovative ultrasound technology to monitor the curing process of 3D printed concrete. This breakthrough allows for precise control over the layering process, ensuring the structural integrity of 3D printed concrete structures.

## Tronix3D Expands 3D Printing Operations in Pittsburgh

[Tronix3D plans $4M expansion of Pittsburgh 3D printing operations](https://www.plasticsnews.com/article/20231128/NEWS/231129907/tronix3d-plans-4m-expansion-of-pittsburgh-3d-printing-operations)

Tronix3D, a leading 3D printing company, is investing $4 million to expand its operations in Pittsburgh. This expansion will create new job opportunities and enhance the company's production capabilities, further solidifying its presence in the 3D printing industry.

## 3D Printing Revolutionizes Denture Creation

[How 3D Printing Is Changing the Future of Dentures](https://www.tomorrowsworldtoday.com/how-3d-printing-is-changing-the-future-of-dentures/)

The dental industry is embracing 3D printing technology to create customized dentures. This method offers faster production times, improved fit, and enhanced aesthetics. The article highlights how 3D printing is transforming the way dentures are made, providing a more comfortable and efficient solution for patients.

## 3D Systems and Precision Resource Collaborate on Metal 3D Printing

[3D Systems and Precision Resource expand metal 3D printing](https://www.engineering.com/story/3d-systems-and-precision-resource-expand-metal-3d-printing)

In a significant partnership, 3D Systems and Precision Resource are joining forces to expand metal 3D printing capabilities. This collaboration aims to provide advanced metal printing solutions, catering to various industries with improved efficiency and precision.

## Army Research Leads to First 3D-Printed Heavy-Fuel Engine

[News - Army research leads to first 3D-printed heavy-fuel engine](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/493423/army-research-leads-first-3d-printed-heavy-fuel-engine)

The U.S. Army has successfully developed the world's first 3D-printed heavy-fuel engine. This groundbreaking achievement showcases the potential of 3D printing in the defense industry, offering new possibilities for efficient and rapid manufacturing of military equipment.

These stories highlight the incredible advancements and diverse applications of 3D printing technology. From construction to healthcare and manufacturing, 3D printing is revolutionizing various sectors, offering innovative solutions and pushing the boundaries of what is possible.
