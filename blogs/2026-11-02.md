# 3D Printing Innovations: From Concrete to Dentistry

The world of 3D printing continues to evolve, offering exciting breakthroughs and applications across various industries. Here are some of the most positive and uplifting stories from today's Google News headlines:

## UNIST Develops Ultrasound Technology for 3D Printed Concrete

[UNIST Uses Ultrasound to Tell When 3D Printed Concrete Can Take the Next Layer](https://3dprinting.com/)

Researchers at UNIST have developed an innovative method to monitor the strength of 3D printed concrete layers using ultrasound. This technology ensures that each layer is strong enough to support the next, leading to more reliable and efficient construction processes.

[Tronix3D plans $4M expansion of Pittsburgh 3D printing operations](https://www.plasticsnews.com/article/20231128/NEWS/231129903/tronix3d-plans-4m-expansion-of-pittsburgh-3d-printing-operations)

Tronix3D is investing $4 million to expand its 3D printing operations in Pittsburgh. This expansion will enhance the company's capabilities and contribute to the growth of the 3D printing industry in the region.

[News - Army research leads to first 3D-printed heavy-fuel engine](https://www.dvidshub.net/news/483880/army-research-leads-first-3d-printed-heavy-fuel-engine)
